# Build My Own AI from Zero

This project is a hands-on initiative focused on understanding how artificial intelligence systems are developed, trained, and evaluated in practice. It explores the fundamental principles behind AI model creation, including how systems learn from data, process information, and generate meaningful outputs for users.

Through this project, we aim to gain deeper insight into the real mechanics of AI, from data acquisition and model training to inference and response generation. We will also examine common challenges in AI systems, such as hallucinations, limitations in reasoning, and the importance of rigorous testing and validation.

The goal is to build a practical understanding of how AI works at a technical level while appreciating the strengths, constraints, and responsibilities involved in creating reliable intelligent systems.
